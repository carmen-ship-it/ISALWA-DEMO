# 1. The 8-minute journey

## Minute 1 — Breath (Pulso)
**She sees:** Login wordmark → **Pulso**. Four vitals only. One Spanish sentence written by the system. Three focus chips.

**She feels:** *Calm power. This is not Excel. This is a cockpit.*

**Narration (presenter):**  
“Esto no es un CRM. Es el pulso de ISALWA. En diez segundos usted sabe si el negocio está sano.”

**Product answers:** Ventas del mes vs meta · Cartera en riesgo · Visitas cumplidas · WhatsApp dentro de SLA — **all computed from the living database**.

**Transition:** Click the sentence / focus chip “Clientes A en silencio”.

---

## Minute 2 — The question she always has (Radar)
**She asks (out loud or in her head):** *“¿Quién necesita mi atención hoy?”*

**She sees:** **Radar** — ranked attention objects, not a task dump.  
Top cards include: Ferretería Don Julio Equipetrol (visita vencida) · Constructora Valle Andino (cartera) · SLA WhatsApp en riesgo.

**She feels:** *Relief. The system is watching when she can’t.*

**Discovery:** “No sabía que podíamos priorizar así.”

**Transition:** Open **Don Julio Equipetrol**.

---

## Minute 3 — The human dossier (Personas / Living Dossier)
**She sees:** Sticky spine — name, segment A/B, score, credit chip, owner Carlos/Lucía, AI briefing in Spanish, next best action.

**Chapters:** Pulso del cliente · Memoria (timeline) · Relación (visitas + mapa) · Comercio · Señal.

**She feels:** *I’ve never seen one customer this clearly.*

**Surprise:** AI summary cites evidence chips (“última compra…”, “saldo…”, “última visita…”).

**Discovery:** “No sabía que un cliente podía tener un briefing así.”

**Transition:** Scroll to **Comercio** → open last prices / nueva cotización.

---

## Minute 4 — Memory is money (Quote Canvas / Cierre)
**She sees:** Quote canvas. Add “Inodoro estándar…”. Whisper appears: **Último precio a este cliente: Bs X · hace N días**. She sends — then accepts — and lands on the living invoice.

**She feels:** *We finally stop inventing prices in WhatsApp.*

**Presenter:** “Cada cotización recuerda la relación. El margen deja de vivir en la cabeza del asesor. Y el cierre genera factura de verdad.”

**Discovery:** “No sabía que podíamos ver el último precio en el momento de cotizar.”

**Transition:** From invoice → payment or jump to **Territorio**.

---

## Minute 5 — The map is the business (Territorio)
**She sees:** Santa Cruz living map — pins with segment/debt state. Heat of activity. Day ribbon for Carlos.

**She feels:** *My company has geography. Of course it does.*

**Surprise:** Click Valle Andino pin → debt glow; Don Julio → silence.

**Discovery:** “No sabía que podíamos mapear a todos los clientes.”

**Transition:** Open **Señal**.

---

## Minute 6 — WhatsApp becomes an instrument (Señal)
**She sees:** Tri-pane runway. Three corporate lines as channels. SLA arc. Dossier docked on the right while reading a thread from Casa Cerámica Oriente.

**She feels:** *Our chaos just got a nervous system.*

**Presenter:** “WhatsApp ya no es un agujero negro. Es medible.”

**Discovery:** “No sabía que el tiempo de respuesta se podía medir.”

**Transition:** Back to Pulso → drill **Cartera en riesgo**.

---

## Minute 7 — Follow the money (Executive drill-down)
**She sees:** From Pulso vital → filtered Radar/Cobranzas lens → Constructora Valle Andino → invoices with balances → payment promise.

**She feels:** *I can confront reality without calling three people.*

**Discovery:** “No sabía que la cobranza y la visita vivían en el mismo lugar.”

**Transition:** ⌘K / Comando — type “cerámica”.

---

## Minute 8 — Mastery (Comando) + close
**She sees:** Command palette finds Casa Cerámica Oriente instantly — score, deuda, último touch. Enter opens dossier. Secondary action: Cotizar.

**She feels:** *I could run the company from this bar.*

**Close line:**  
“Todo lo que vimos — pulso, riesgo, mapa, WhatsApp, precios, visitas — es el mismo modelo de datos. No es una demo falsa. Es ISALWA OS.”

**Target reaction:** *“Necesito esto.” / “No sabía que el software podía funcionar así.”*

# 3. Ten signature moments (why each deserves to exist)

1. **Pulso opens** — Replaces “¿cómo vamos?” meetings. If this fails, nothing else matters.  
2. **System sentence** — Turns metrics into a human briefing. Owners don’t want charts; they want meaning.  
3. **Radar triage** — Proves the OS watches the business overnight.  
4. **Living Dossier spine** — Kills tabbed CRM death. One customer, one truth.  
5. **AI account briefing with evidence** — Magic that still feels honest.  
6. **Last-price whisper in Quote Canvas** — Directly protects margin; unforgettable for distributors.  
7. **Territorio heatmap** — Makes a manufacturer feel modern and in control of the street.  
8. **Señal SLA arc** — Transforms WhatsApp from anxiety into an instrument.  
9. **Cartera drill-down** — Connects emotion (worry about cash) to action.  
10. **Comando (⌘K)** — The “of course” moment power users and owners both feel.

# 5. Presenter rules
