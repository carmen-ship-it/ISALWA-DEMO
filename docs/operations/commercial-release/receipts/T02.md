## 3. Qué cambió

### 3.1 Decisión aplicada (2026-10-16 17:18, literal)

"Customer contact details follow customer/account visibility, enforced server-side consistently in list, detail, search, autocomplete, counts, exports, conversations, and related endpoints. Allow: - Current account owner. - Advisor with active, applicable coverage. - Team lead with commercial.team.read over that account owner's team. - Actor with explicit commercial.org.read. - Other operational roles only through an existing explicit permission and the minimum customer information needed for their authorized task. Active membership alone and people.admin alone do not grant commercial contact visibility. Customer search must follow the same boundary; do not preserve a broad endpoint as a workaround. Conversation authorship alone must not provide permanent access after account reassignment, coverage expiration, or revocation. Require current resource authority. Preserve the author in history without granting access. For an unlinked provisional conversation, allow the currently authorized handler or explicitly authorized intake queue. Do not make historical authorship the fallback permission. Do not add company-wide customer discovery or new grants in this task. Duplicate checks must not disclose restricted customer contact details; return a generic review-required outcome when necessary. Add HTTP/Postgres evidence for direct contact endpoints, search, reassignment, expired/revoked coverage, suspended membership, author-only access, and cross-tenant IDs. Verify response bodies and zero unauthorized effects—not just status codes. Continue T02 under this decision. Record any affected operational dependency rather than silently widening permissions."
