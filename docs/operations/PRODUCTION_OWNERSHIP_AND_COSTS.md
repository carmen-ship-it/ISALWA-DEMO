# Production ownership and costs — ISALWA OS

**Status:** DOCUMENTED planning register. Not a bill. Not a migration.  
**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Lane:** L (ownership and cost boundary only)

**Business truth stays on managed PostgreSQL** (`OS_DATABASE_URL`). Supabase Auth stores login credentials only. Architect Postgres, demo databases, and a personal laptop are not the system of record.

Dollar figures below are **bands from existing plans or policy caps**. They are **not confirmed invoices**. Flag: **confirm with the provider before budgeting**.

Desired owner for every production account: the company (ISALWA legal entity), with **two** people who can pay, recover access, and rotate secrets. Current staging evidence is still personal.

- Clerk, WorkOS, Redis, a separate worker queue, and a map SDK are out of this ownership pass (`PRODUCTION_STAGING_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §19).
