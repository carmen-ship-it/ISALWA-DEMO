# What is ISALWA Architect?

A business architecture platform that discovers how a company operates, understands its processes, identifies bottlenecks, designs the future operating model, and prepares implementation-ready software architecture.

It sits with the business before a single line of production software is written.

It listens. It remembers. It structures. It recommends — only when evidence allows.

It is the first phase of every custom software project.

---

## It is NOT

- a chatbot
- a CRM
- a workflow editor
- a project management tool
- a generic AI assistant
- a form that pretends to be consulting
- a place to “generate apps” without understanding the company

If a feature would make Architect feel like any of the above, it does not belong.

---

# Vision

In five years, ISALWA Architect is the world’s best business discovery and software planning platform.

Owners and operators open it and feel understood — not surveyed.

Consultants open it and feel armed — not replaced.

Engineers open it and receive clarity — not chaos.

The market recognizes Architect as the standard first step before custom software: the place where companies get a living blueprint of how they work, what is broken, what to build, and why.

Not the loudest AI product.  
The calmest, most precise one.

# Product Principles

> **Long-term principles (DO NOT REMOVE)** — override individual missions:  
> [`apps/architect/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md`](../apps/architect/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md)

- **Evidence over opinions** — Recommendations earn their place. Speculation does not.
- **Understand before recommending** — Diagnosis precedes prescription.
- **One source of truth** — Memory, Knowledge, Blueprint, Solution, Processes, Deliverables. No parallel realities.
- **Explain every recommendation** — Why. Evidence. Confidence. Always.
- **Human-first consulting** — The product thinks like a senior consultant sitting across the table.
- **Premium experience** — The feeling of Apple, Linear, and a McKinsey partner — never a generic SaaS template.
- **Calm software** — Soft motion. Clear hierarchy. No noise.
- **Business first** — Departments, processes, people, and information flow before screens and stacks.
- **Technology second** — Architecture serves the business model, not the other way around.
- **Never overwhelm the client** — One screen can tell the truth. Ten competing panels cannot.
- **Every recommendation must trace back to evidence** — If it cannot be traced, it cannot be shown.

# Consulting Principles

- Always ask why.
- Never assume.
- Discover bottlenecks.
- Think in systems.
- Think in departments.
- Think in processes.
- Think in people.
- Think in information flow.

A good question is worth more than a premature module recommendation.

Consequence thinking matters:

> Earlier you mentioned only one person knows purchasing.  
> If that employee resigned tomorrow… what would happen?

That is consultant thinking. That is Architect.

# Product Boundaries

ISALWA Architect will **never** become:

- an ERP replacement
- a Slack / Teams replacement
- a ChatGPT clone
- a no-code / low-code builder
- a workflow automation platform
- a generic BI tool
- a ticket tracker
- a document dump with AI on top

Architect discovers, structures, designs, and prepares.  
ISALWA OS and engineering teams build and operate.

Clarity of boundary is a feature.

# Decision Filter

Every future feature must answer:

**Does this make the Architect better at understanding businesses?**

If not…

**Don’t build it.**

If it only makes the product busier, prettier without insight, or more “AI” without evidence — reject it.

Understanding is the north star.  
Everything else is orbit.

# How to use this document

Read this before proposing a mission, a feature, or a redesign.
