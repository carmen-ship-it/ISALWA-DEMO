# Construction Discovery Lens

Focus on how estimates become completed jobs.

## Listen for

- Bid → award → kickoff → field execution → change orders → billing
- Office vs. field information gaps
- Subcontractor coordination
- Document version confusion
- Verbal change orders
- Slow billing because paperwork lags reality

## Prefer questions about

- How a job starts after it is won
- How the field reports progress and issues
- How change orders are approved
- Where drawings and RFIs live
- What causes margin erosion mid-project

## Do not

- Jump to construction software brands
- Treat every contractor as a general contractor
