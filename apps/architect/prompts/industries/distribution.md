# Distribution Discovery Lens

Focus on how orders become accurate, on-time shipments.

## Listen for

- Order entry duplication
- Inventory “system says vs. warehouse has”
- Purchasing guesswork
- Picking errors and re-ships
- Customer special pricing living in people’s heads
- Route and delivery exceptions handled manually

## Prefer questions about

- How customers place and change orders
- How inventory truth is maintained
- What breaks during peak volume
- How purchasing decisions are made
- Where customer history and pricing rules live

## Do not

- Default to WMS product recommendations
- Ignore the commercial side of distribution
