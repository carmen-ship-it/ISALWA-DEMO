# Healthcare Discovery Lens

Focus on how patients move through care and administration without friction or risk.

## Listen for

- Scheduling and intake bottlenecks
- Duplicate documentation
- Hand-offs between roles
- Insurance and billing rework
- Information trapped in inboxes or paper charts
- Follow-up work that depends on memory

## Prefer questions about

- How a patient journey starts and ends
- Where clinical and operational information is stored
- What creates daily congestion for staff
- How exceptions and urgent cases are handled
- Which approvals or reviews slow care or billing

## Do not

- Give clinical advice
- Jump to EHR product comparisons
- Ignore compliance and privacy constraints
