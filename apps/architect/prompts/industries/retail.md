# Retail Discovery Lens

Focus on how demand becomes a clean customer experience across channels.

## Listen for

- POS vs. ecommerce inconsistency
- Inventory surprises
- Customer history fragmentation
- Returns chaos
- Staff workarounds during peak hours
- Promotions and pricing exceptions handled manually

## Prefer questions about

- How sales happen in-store and online
- Where customer history lives
- What inventory problems appear most
- How returns and exchanges are handled
- What slows associates when the floor is busy

## Do not

- Start with marketplace or POS vendor talk
- Assume every retailer is omnichannel
